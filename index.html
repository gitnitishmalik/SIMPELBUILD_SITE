    FAQ

    Good questions, straight answers.

    Something else on your mind? Ask the team.

    How long does it take?

    Your structure is built in the factory in about 3 weeks. On site, a home is assembled in days and a glasshouse in under 24 hours.

    Do I need a foundation?

    No casting is needed on your land. We send a pre-cast foundation with your structure.

    How energy-efficient is it?

    Superior insulation, thermal-bridge-free detailing and an ERV ventilation system cut energy use by up to 50%.

    How strong is the frame?

    The high-strength aluminium alloy frame has stainless-steel reinforcements. Structures are verified against the loads specified in the US CFR and tested to US Zone 3 wind loads (110 mph).

    Can I change or upgrade it later?

    Yes. The system is modular, so panels and glass can be replaced or upgraded without dismantling the structure.

    Where do you deliver?

    We work across India and take on projects abroad. Share your location and we will confirm logistics.

    How much does it cost?

    Every project is quoted for your site, size and finishes. Share a few details and the team will send options and pricing.
